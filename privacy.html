Privacy

What we collect, and why.

This is a short, plain-language description of how information submitted through this site is handled. It applies to forms, emails, and any other communication initiated through these pages. Because membership in the community is private, this notice matters more here than on most sites: what you send us stays with us.

What we collect

The application and contact forms collect only what you choose to submit: name, email, the city you intend to serve, an approximate target year, and your written answers. We do not require accounts, passwords, or any identifying document.

How we use it

Submissions are read only by us and by the Review Panel, and used solely to consider and respond to your application or message. We do not sell, rent, or share the contents of inquiries with any third party. We will not enroll you in any newsletter or list as a result of submitting a form.

How we store it

Submissions are stored in a private email inbox and a private records system. We retain applications indefinitely so that we can pick up conversations months or years later as your plans evolve — including reapplications after a “not this year” outcome. You may ask us to delete your records at any time by writing to us via the contact form.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses minimal first-party analytics to count page views in aggregate. No third-party advertising cookies are placed by us. If we add analytics in the future, this notice will be updated accordingly.

Updates

If this notice changes in any material way, the updated text will appear here.
